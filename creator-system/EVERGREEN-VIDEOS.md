# Evergreen Video Bank

Videos that are as useful in 6-12 months as the day they publish. The trick: teach **workflows and principles with tools as examples**, never tool tours. Name the tool once, teach the job forever. When a tool dies, the video survives because the job didn't change.

Rules for staying evergreen:
- Frame around the JOB ("write listings in 10 minutes"), not the TOOL ("ChatGPT 5.2 tutorial")
- Say "whatever AI you use — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, doesn't matter" early; it future-proofs and widens the audience
- No version numbers in titles. No "new," "just dropped," "2026" unless you WANT it dated
- Demo on screen with one tool, but teach the prompt/workflow pattern that transfers

## The ratio
Per month: ~3 evergreen + ~1 timely news-peg video. Evergreen compounds on search for years; the news peg tests whether you can ride a wave. The trend brief supplies the peg; this bank supplies everything else — you never start a batch day with a blank page.
