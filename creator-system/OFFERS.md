# 10 Email Offers for Real Estate Agents

Each one is a lead magnet: you mention it in a video ("comment SEND / link in the description and I'll email it to you"), it lands in their inbox, they're on the list. One offer per video. Each is buildable in under 2 hours with AI — most are prompt packs + a one-page PDF. Build them the week you need them, not in advance (14-day rule).

| # | Offer | What it actually is | The line you say on camera |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The 10-Minute Listing Machine** | Prompt pack: paste MLS facts + 3 photos' descriptions → listing description, Instagram caption, email blast, and a "just listed" text message. One prompt each, copy-paste ready. | "Ten minutes from MLS sheet to every piece of marketing the listing needs. I'll send you the exact prompts." |
| 2 | **48 Prompts Agents Actually Use** | Swipe file organized by job: prospecting, listings, buyers, follow-up, social. Each prompt has a filled-in example. | "Not 'write me a poem about real estate' — 48 prompts for the stuff you did today." |
| 3 | **The Follow-Up Engine** | 6-email nurture sequence templates (open house leads, expired, sphere) + the prompt that personalizes each one from a contact's notes in 30 seconds. | "The money's in the follow-up you're not sending. Here's the sequence plus the AI that writes it for you." |
| 4 | **The Buyer-Consult Briefing** | One prompt that builds a neighborhood + market one-pager before any showing: schools, comps trend, days-on-market, talking points. | "Walk into every consult looking like you spent an hour prepping. You spent ninety seconds." |
| 5 | **The Objection Gym** | Role-play prompt: AI plays the FSBO / the 'we'll wait for rates' buyer / the commission-cutter, you practice out loud, it critiques you. | "Practice the conversation before it costs you the deal. Your AI sparring partner — free." |
| 6 | **The 30-Day Content Calendar** | Prompt that generates a month of local-market social posts from one input (your farm area) + a template for turning each into a 30-second phone video. | "Thirty days of posts, one prompt, your market. Stop staring at the blank caption box." |
| 7 | **The Plain-English Deal Decoder** | Prompts that translate inspection reports and addenda into client-friendly summaries (with the 'I'm not your lawyer/inspector' guardrail language built in). | "Your client got a 40-page inspection report at 9pm. Send them clarity in five minutes." |
| 8 | **The Review Machine** | Post-closing review-request scripts (text + email), timed sequence, plus the prompt that personalizes each ask from the deal's story. | "You did great work. Google shows nine reviews. Fix the gap on autopilot." |
| 9 | **The CMA Storyteller** | Prompt that turns raw comps into a narrative sellers actually understand — why the number is the number, in their language. | "The CMA is right; the seller just didn't feel it. Here's how AI makes the number land." |
| 10 | **The Sunday Hour** | One-page weekly workflow: 60 minutes every Sunday, AI preps your entire week (follow-ups drafted, content queued, consults briefed). The system, not a tool. | "One hour on Sunday and AI runs your admin week. This is the exact checklist I'd give my own agent." |

## Notes
- **#8 is the MissedMe bridge** — it can end with "and if you'd rather never think about it, that's literally what my company does." Use sparingly; the list is the asset, the pitch is occasional.
- **#1, #2, #10 are launch-tier** — broadest appeal, use them for videos 1-3.
- **#5 and #7 make the best video demos** — watching AI role-play a difficult seller is inherently entertaining; do the demo on camera, the offer converts itself.
- Every offer doubles as a video topic ("I built an AI that argues with you like a FSBO — free below").
- Compliance note on #7: keep the disclaimer language in the PDF; never position AI output as inspection/legal advice on camera.
