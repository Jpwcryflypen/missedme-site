# LinkedIn Profile — Two-Phase Plan

**Constraint:** John is still at CoStar and needs the profile to keep reading that way — no "started a new position" cards, no side-income signals to a network full of CoStar colleagues. Phase 0 is what goes live now. Phase 1 is the full version, ready to swap the day the constraint lifts.

---

## Phase 0 — Stealth mode (use this NOW)

### Do this BEFORE editing anything (2 minutes)
1. **Settings & Privacy → Visibility → "Share profile updates with your network" → OFF.** This is the master switch — with it off, LinkedIn doesn't broadcast profile edits.
2. **Do NOT add any new Experience entry** (no MissedMe, no "Content Creator"). New positions are the #1 thing that generates network notification cards, and the Experience section is the first place people check employment status.
3. CoStar stays exactly as-is: current role, present tense, untouched.
4. Headline and About edits are safe — they don't generate "new position" announcements, and with the switch off they don't broadcast at all.

### Rules of the road while at CoStar
- **Nothing for sale on LinkedIn.** No Skool links, no workshop offers, no "DM me to work together." Free resources only ("comment and I'll send it") — generosity reads as professional sharing, not moonlighting. Monetization lives in YouTube descriptions + email, where the CoStar network isn't looking.
- Content = "what I'm testing and learning," never "what I sell/teach clients." Same videos, softer frame.
- Don't link the YouTube channel in Featured yet if the channel name/branding reads like a business. A personal-name channel is fine to feature later.
- ⚠ Given the active litigation, worth 10 minutes re-reading the employment agreement's outside-activities/moonlighting clause before the first PAID workshop (not before posting content — sharing what you're learning is not a side business). Not legal advice, just sequencing.
