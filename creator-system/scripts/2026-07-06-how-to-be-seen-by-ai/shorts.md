## Short 2 — "Your listing sounds like a robot wrote it (it did)"
*Niche, evergreen, full-authority lane (listing copy).*

**First frame:** John holding his phone up like he's reading a listing, mid eye-roll. On-screen text: "'Nestled in the heart of...'"

**Text-on-screen (beat by beat):**
"BUYERS CAN SMELL CHATGPT" → "THE 2-LINE FIX" → "Line 1: ban the clichés" → "Line 2: one real detail"

**Script (~78 words):**

"Nestled in the heart of." Stop. Buyers can smell ChatGPT.

Nestled. Boasts. Oasis. Must-see. That's not your listing, that's the default setting.

The fix is two lines in your prompt.

Line one: "Banned words: nestled, boasts, oasis, must-see, won't last long."

Line two: give it one detail only you know. The kitchen gets morning light. The neighbor has a lemon tree.

The clichés die, the real house shows up, and your listing stops sounding like everyone else's.

It's called tokenmaxxing, and it has a Wikipedia page now. Leaderboards for AI usage went up, and employees did the obvious thing: they gamed the number. Usage exploded. Actual output, not so much.

Amazon already killed its leaderboard.

Measure the work and people do work. Measure the tokens and congratulations, you've invented a token factory.
