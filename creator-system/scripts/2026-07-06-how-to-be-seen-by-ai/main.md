## FAQ DRILL
### 5 questions a skeptical agent would ask about this video

**1. "You're not even an agent. Why are you telling me how to get clients?"**
Because this isn't the transaction side, it's the visibility side, and I've spent 15 years inside your listings from behind the camera. And you don't have to trust me: run the demo yourself. Ask four AIs who the best agent in your market is and click the sources. The machine will tell you the same thing I just did.

**2. "Is that 6-in-10 stat even real?"**
It's one vendor benchmark, from an AI-SEO company, which is why I said "industry benchmark covered by NAR and HousingWire" and not "NAR study." Independent data points the same way: BrightLocal has ChatGPT local-business recommendations going from 6% to 45% in a year. Argue the decimals if you want, the direction isn't close.

**3. "Isn't this just SEO with a new name? I already pay a guy for that."**
It overlaps, but it's not the same game. Only about 11% of cited domains overlap between AI engines, and they lean on reviews, directories, and Reddit in ways classic SEO doesn't. Ask your guy if he's touched your schema, your review spread, or a stat-rich local page. If he stares at you, comment SEEN.

**4. "Why wouldn't I just do the Reddit comment thing? Sounds easy."**
Because the FTC's fake-review rule covers exactly that, Reddit is actively hunting it, and if you get outed astroturfing your own market's subreddit, you're done in that sub forever. I showed it so you know it exists, not so you'd do it. The white-hat version, real name and disclosed, gets you the same mechanism with none of the fallout.

**5. "So should I cancel my Premier Agent spend?"**
That's a budget and lead-strategy call, and I'm the camera guy, not your broker. What I can give you is the facts: Wharton found 99.7% of consumers think Contact Agent reaches the listing agent, and even the vendor data shows Zillow's share of discovery falling from 41% to 34%. You know your numbers. You do that math, not me.
