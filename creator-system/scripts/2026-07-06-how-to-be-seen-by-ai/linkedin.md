## 2. Text post — the Wharton 99.7% stat (Thu)

*Frame: neutral "I did not know this" share. No editorializing beyond the study itself, no employer-adjacent sniping, no CTA. Link to the study goes in the first comment (SSRN 6140990, covered by Fox Business), never in the body.*

I've photographed real estate for 15 years and I did not know this.

A controlled experiment by Wharton emeritus professor Jerry Wind looked at what happens when consumers click "Contact Agent" on a Zillow listing.

99.7% of them believed they were contacting the listing agent. The one whose name is on the sign, who knows the house.

They're actually routed to agents paying for the lead. The study's words: the interface "systematically deceives consumers."

I'm not drawing a conclusion here, the study draws its own. But if you buy, sell, or work in housing, it's worth knowing what that button does. Study linked in the comments.

Were you aware of this? Genuine question, because almost nobody I've asked was.

## 3. Text post — the 4-AI test story (Tue)

*Frame: first-person experiment, learning out loud. No CTA, end on a question.*

Last week I asked four AIs the same question: "Who's the best buyer's agent in Phoenix for first-time buyers?"

ChatGPT gave me one set of names. Perplexity gave me another. Gemini, a third. Claude, a fourth. Barely any overlap, and that tracks with the research: only about 11% of cited web domains are shared between AI engines.

Then I did the part most people skip. I clicked the citations.

Every answer was built from the same kinds of pages. Directory "best of" lists. Review aggregates. A couple of agent blogs with actual local data on them.

Not one AI had an opinion about who's good. They just had sources, and they repeated whatever the sources agreed on.

Which means the agents showing up in those answers aren't better. They're findable.

I keep thinking about how many genuinely great agents I've worked with over 15 years of shoots who would fail this test completely.

Have you ever asked an AI about your own business? What came back?
