Exact Process

Do this every time
1

Pick the right lead

Start with score 65+, non-chain, has phone, critical/high GBP need, and no website or obvious website weakness. Open Maps before calling.

2

Open with the visible gap

Do not say you are a marketing agency first. Mention their review count, city, or missing website. Make it feel observed, not scripted.

3

Get owner context

Ask if they are new in town or how long they have been around. This creates the math frame without accusing them of having weak reviews.

4

Ask only math questions

Jobs per month, years in business, review count, current review process, CRM/job system. No random discovery wandering.

5

State the review leak

Use the low end of their job count. Show that they likely served hundreds or thousands of customers but captured only a small slice as reviews.

6

Offer the review trial

Two-week trial. Use their past customers or completed jobs. After trial, 97 bucks/month to keep review capture running. If no list, sell forward capture only.

7

Do not over-explain tools

They do not care about GHL, automations, or widgets yet. Say it requests reviews consistently and tracks what happens.

8

Upsell after proof

On day 6-10, when reviews move, bridge to GBP visibility and WordPress: stronger reviews matter more when the profile and site convert.

Follow-Up Rules

Keep it simple
A

No answer

Call again at a different day/time. Do not judge the list from 10 dials. Track owner conversations, not pickups.

B

Gatekeeper

Ask when the owner is usually in or whether they handle the Google profile. Keep it light. Do not pitch the whole offer to a receptionist.

C

Send info

Send one specific line about their profile, not a brochure. Attach a placeholder callback time so the thread has a next step.

D

Trial interested

Next step is customer list/completed jobs source, Google review link, payment link, and setup call. Do not start custom work without payment flow.

E

Day 6-10 upsell

Show review movement, then ask whether they want the profile and website fixed so those reviews turn into more trust and calls.
