Straight answers

Is this against Google's rules?

No. We only ask your real customers about real jobs you actually completed. Every customer gets the same ask — no filtering, no incentives, no fake or purchased reviews, ever. That stuff gets profiles suspended, and we don't touch it.

Do I have to do anything?

Send us your customer list and approve the message we'll send. That's it. You keep doing the work — we handle the rest. As new jobs wrap up, those customers get the same ask, so it keeps running without you thinking about it.

What if I don't have a customer list?

Plenty of companies run on paper invoices and a phone full of texts — that's fine. We'll help you pull a list together from what you've got: old invoices, texts, emails, job notes. Then we set up a dead-simple way to capture every customer going forward. Not having a list isn't a dealbreaker — it's usually the first thing we fix.

What if a customer had a bad experience?

It happens to every company. We flag responses so you can follow up personally — most unhappy customers just want to be heard, and a good follow-up often turns them around. What we won't do is hide them from the ask. Every real customer gets the same invitation.

What does it cost?

It's a simple monthly service with no contract — and your first 25 reviews are free either way. Leave your info below and we'll walk you through it.
