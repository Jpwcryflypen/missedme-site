Straight answers

Before you spend anything

Do you build the automation as part of the assessment?

No. The assessment diagnoses, prioritizes, and recommends. If you want help implementing a recommendation, we will define and price that work separately before starting.

What counts as projected time savings?

We start with the frequency and time you report for a recurring workflow, then estimate the portion a practical change could reasonably reduce. The report shows those assumptions so you can challenge them.

What if my business is not a fit?

We will say so. A business needs enough recurring digital or administrative work for the chosen package. If the paid assessment cannot identify the package threshold, the guarantee described above applies.

Are the recommendations always “AI tools”?

No. Sometimes the right answer is a better process, a feature in software you already own, or a simple automation. The goal is useful improvement—not forcing AI into every problem.

Is missed-call text-back already included?

No. Call routing, texting, consent, carrier registration, message handling, and usage costs must be scoped for each business. We will not represent text-back as live until it has been configured and tested.

How are payments handled?

Approved assessments are paid through Stripe-hosted checkout. MissedMe does not receive or store your full card number. Delivery timing and the final scope are confirmed before payment.
