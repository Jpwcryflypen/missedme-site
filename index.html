Common questions

What the free assessment includes

What happens during the 15-minute assessment?

We ask about the problem you want to solve, how it works today, and what it costs in time or missed opportunities. We will tell you the first change we would consider.

Do I have to buy anything?

No. If there is useful paid work, we will explain what it would involve. You can take the recommendation, think about it, or handle it yourself.

Can MissedMe build what you recommend?

Yes, when the work fits what we do. We will define the deliverables, timing, and price in writing before starting.

Can you guarantee a certain number of reviews or leads?

No. We can build and run the agreed process, but Google, customer behavior, demand, and the quality of the business all affect the result.

Is AI call answering ready for every business?

No. The call flow, business information, calendar, handoff rules, and phone setup have to be configured and tested for each business.

How are payments handled?

The 15-minute assessment is free. Paid work uses Stripe-hosted checkout after the scope and price have been confirmed in writing. MissedMe does not receive or store your full card number.
