    Exact Process

    Use on every call
    1

    Confirm it is a real operator

    Open Maps and the website. Confirm the service category, city, active phone, and that it is not a call-center duplicate.

    2

    Use one observed fact

    Lead with the visible review count or a confirmed website issue. Never claim the website is bad when it only failed an automated check.

    3

    Reach the owner

    Ask whether the owner or person handling Google is available. Track owner conversations separately from pickups.

    4

    Ask for unique-customer math

    Ask years in business and unique customers or households per month. Do not count repeat visits as separate review opportunities.

    5

    Diagnose the capture gap

    Ask how every eligible customer is requested today, what system triggers it, and whether follow-up happens consistently.

    6

    Offer a fixed-scope trial

    Use genuine recent customers, neutral requests, no incentives, no rating filters, and clear messaging costs and limits.

    7

    Define success before launch

    Track eligible contacts, deliveries, clicks, reviews received, and opt-outs. Do not guarantee a review count or ranking.

    8

    Upsell only after proof

    When review capture works, show the next verified profile or website constraint and offer the larger GBP or WordPress scope separately.

    Compliance Gate

    Before activation
    A

    Real customer experience

    Only request reviews from customers who genuinely received the service.

    B

    Neutral solicitation

    Do not offer incentives, gate by satisfaction, suppress negative feedback, or request a specific rating or wording.

    C

    Messaging authorization

    Confirm the client's customer-data authority, sender identity, opt-out handling, and applicable SMS/email requirements before uploading a list.

    D

    Clear commercial terms

    State trial start, included contacts, usage costs, renewal choice, cancellation method, and separate custom-work pricing in writing.

    E

    Honor Do Not Call

    Record every opt-out immediately. A verified mobile line identifies routing, not permission to text or repeatedly call.
