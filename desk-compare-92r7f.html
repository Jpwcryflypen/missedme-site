What the Terms Mean

Opportunity score

Higher means more visible problems you can sell against: weak review position, incomplete Google Profile, or website problems. It does not measure profitability.

Call ready

The listing is an independent service business with a callable number, acceptable rating, and a measurable sales opportunity. It does not predict a sale.

Traditional-trade average

The average result from HVAC, plumbing, and landscaping businesses matched to the same cities and business counts as the niche being tested.
